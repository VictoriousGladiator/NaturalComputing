Q2: Collective migration (lower activity)

Reproduction of the self-study exercise where cells keep moving even at higher densities. This variant uses a lower activity persistence (see MAX_ACT in the config).

To explore the density effect, adjust NRCELLS in the config and reload.

Observations

  • Cells still tend to cluster even when cell-cell adhesion is set to 0 (here: J[1][1] = 0). In practice, the motion becomes constrained by neighbours and cells can "jam" in the direction of their activity, forming a few relatively stable clusters.
  • This clustering can persist at higher seeding counts: newly seeded cells in empty space often wander for a while and then join an existing cluster. Occasionally, a cell leaves a cluster and later reconnects, which can lead to clusters merging into a larger one.
  • At near-confluent densities, the system tends to form one larger connected structure. Edge cells still probe around, but generally remain attached to the main cohort.
