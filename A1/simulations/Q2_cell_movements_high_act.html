Q2: Collective migration (higher activity)

Reproduction of the self-study exercise where cells keep moving even at higher densities. This variant uses a higher activity persistence (see MAX_ACT in the config).

To explore the density effect, adjust NRCELLS in the config and reload.

Observations

  • Cells still form clusters, but these clusters are less stationary: they tend to translate together and can push in a common direction for some time, producing more coherent "streams" of motion.
  • Compared to the lower-activity case, clusters are less stable: collisions and break-ups happen more often. The stronger driving force reduces how quickly the system settles into long-lived, stable cohorts.
  • At very high densities, limited free space eventually forces the population into a mostly connected mass anyway. Any remaining "hole" of empty space tends to drift around as cells flow past and rearrange.
