The method is based on checking length-n substrings, so if the input is shorter than n there are no such substrings to match and the score becomes trivial and uninformative. 
If the input is much longer, it contains many more length-n substrings, 
which creates many more chances for matches and makes the score grow mainly with length rather than with how English-like the content is, 
so scores across examples stop being comparable unless you normalize for length.
